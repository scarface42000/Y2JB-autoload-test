Y2JB Payload Manager autoload patch (experimental)

Changes:
- Adds Payload_Manager_v0.6.3.elf to the YouTube download0 cache assets.
- After Relapse reports successful completion, Relapse.js attempts to connect to 127.0.0.1:9021 and stream the ELF to elfldr.
- Keeps the original Relapse, kexp, and elfldr files unchanged.

Important limitations:
- This is a modified SOURCE ZIP, not a rebuilt download0.dat UFS2 image.
- The code has not been tested on a physical PS5; use at your own risk and keep your original download0.dat.
- This only autoloads Payload Manager after Y2JB/Relapse is run. It does not trigger the jailbreak automatically at console power-on.
- The target firmware is PS5 13.60 as supplied by the user; successful execution depends on the user's exact app/version and source image.
